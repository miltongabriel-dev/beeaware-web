Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account to use BeeAware?

No. You can view the map and report incidents anonymously without creating an account. Signing in (with Google, Apple, or an email code) unlocks extra features such as saving an emergency contact and managing your reports.

Is BeeAware an emergency service?

No. BeeAware is not an emergency service and does not replace the police, ambulance, or fire department. If you are in immediate danger, always contact your local emergency number first.

How do I delete my account or data?

Email info@jagolabs.tech with the subject line "Delete my account" and we will process your request within 30 days. See our Privacy Policy for details.
